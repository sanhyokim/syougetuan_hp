A word from the kitchen

We pack these boxes in our own kitchen, to be opened on the first morning of the year.

Fish from the Seto Inland Sea and ingredients from Hiroshima, each prepared with the same thinking as our omakase.

On the afternoon of New Year's Eve, the noren will be up and we will be waiting for you.

The chef

What is inside

Two tiers, 21 dishes in all.

First tier

Celebratory starters

  • Tamba black beans in syrup
  • Herring roe in white soy
  • Candied small sardines
  • Pounded burdock with sesame

Sweet-savoury bites

  • Rolled sweet omelette
  • Chestnuts in sweet potato
  • Red-and-white daikon with Hiroshima lemonHiroshima lemons, peel and all, so the fragrance does the work of the vinegar.
  • Glazed kuruma prawns
  • Lily-bulb chakin
  • Roast duck breast

Second tier

Grilled

  • Winter Spanish mackerel, yuan-yakiMackerel from the winter Seto Inland Sea, marinated with yuzu before grilling.
  • Winter yellowtail teriyaki
  • Miyajima conger eel rolled around burdockConger eel landed near Miyajima, wrapped around burdock and grilled.
  • Roast Hiba beefBeef raised in Shobara, northern Hiroshima, roasted slowly at a low heat.

Simmered

  • Hiroshima oysters, simmeredOysters from Hiroshima Bay, simmered briefly so they stay plump.
  • Nishime, simmered vegetables (ebi-imo taro, arrowhead, shiitake, plum-blossom carrot, koya tofu, snow peas)

Please note

CancellationCancellation is free until Sunday 20 December. After that date, the full price is charged.

If you are running lateIf you may be late, please call us. If we have not heard from you by 3:00 pm, the full price is charged, as we cannot keep a chilled box at the restaurant beyond that time.

ReturnsEach box is finished on the morning of collection and must be kept chilled. As we cannot know how it is stored once it leaves us, we cannot accept returns or exchanges.
